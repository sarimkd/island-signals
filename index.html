about this project

A Pacific freshwater investigation

Built for the Pacific DataViz Challenge by Sarim Khan.

I chose an explorable village because freshwater security is lived locally. The story follows a shared ocean signal into different rainfall records, unequal safe-water access and an uneven observing network.

3D models and environment assets are from Kenney asset packs. Data visualizations use the official challenge datasets.

Additional dataset: Pacific Community SDG 6 water and sanitation data, using nine territory reports from 2024 on domestic wastewater safely treated.

Context: Pacific Community coastal aquifer work.

Peer-reviewed research: White, Falkland and Redfern (2024) and Souter et al. (2024).
